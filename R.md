Problem Statement: Employee Leave Management System
- Develop an Employee Leave Management System using Zoho Creator to automate employee leave applications, approvals, and tracking.
- Forms: Create two forms to collect employee details and leave requests.
- Basic Fields: Use single-line, email, phone, date, and dropdown fields to store employee and leave information.
- Advanced Fields: Use lookup, formula, subform, and other suitable advanced fields to manage related records and calculate leave duration.
- Deluge Scripting: Implement 2–3 Deluge scripts to validate leave dates, calculate leave days, and update leave statuses.
- Workflow Automation: Configure a workflow to trigger notifications or actions when a leave request is submitted or its status changes.
- Dataset Import: Import employee and leave records from a CSV or Excel dataset.
- AI Features: Incorporate suitable AI-powered fields or features available in Zoho Creator to assist with leave-related data processing.
- Reports and Dashboards: Display employee details, pending requests, approved leaves, and leave summaries.
- Expected Outcome: Reduce manual work, improve data accuracy, and simplify employee leave management through automation.
